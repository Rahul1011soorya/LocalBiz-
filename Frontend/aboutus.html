About LocalBiz Connect

A simple full-stack learning project for begineers

Project Idea

LocalBiz Connect helps a small business display services online and cinnect enquiries from customers.

Why We Are Building This

This project helps students understand how a real web project grows: HTML structure,CSS design,JavaScript interaction,Backend API,database and final submission.

Future Scope

In later days,the contact form can be connected to Flask backend and SQLite database.Then the dashboard can show real enquiry data.
